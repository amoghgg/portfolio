Where machine perception breaks.

Why the stage

Somewhere right now a camera is watching a face. It follows a hand, reads an expression, squeezes a whole person into a handful of numbers and draws them again at the other end of a call. Almost all of it was built for someone sitting still, in good light, doing nothing much.

I learned to watch people in rehearsal rooms, and a stage is the opposite of sitting still. One eyebrow turns a line into a question. The lights fight every camera in the room. Miss one moment and the scene goes with it. So that is where I take these systems: somewhere they have to perform. I push until they break, and then I look at who gets hurt.
