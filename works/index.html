Made for one person at a time.

What these are

The same question as the research, but this time you are the one in front of the camera. Pixel Manifold turns your face into points of light, right in your browser. Lores is the photo tool this whole site's look grew out of. Talking is a mirror that hands you my face.

The list

Three pieces
  1. 01 Pixel Manifold Your face and hands become 1,298 points of light. Open your hands, and the whole thing bursts into colour. In your browserNeeds a webcam 2026 →
  2. 02 Lores Film looks and proper pixel art for your photos and videos, including Fujifilm's film simulations. Nothing leaves your browser. There is a Mac app too, with AI photo edits that run on your own machine. Toollores.amoghbajpai.com 2026 ↗
  3. 03 Talking A live mirror. The room, your clothes and your hands stay yours. Only your face slowly becomes mine. A learning re-build of Aisling Phelan's Surrogate Bodies, made to run live on a MacBook. InstallationVideo · runs on a Mac 2026 →
