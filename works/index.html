Things to try

Made for one person at a time.

What these are

The same question as the research, but with you in front of the camera. Most run in your browser: your face turns into light, gets spliced with a copy of itself, or gets described back to you by an AI. The last one is a live installation that gives you my face.

The list

Most of these need your webcam
  1. 01 Pixel Manifold Your face and hands become 1,298 points of light. Open your hands and the whole thing bursts into colour. In your browserNeeds a webcam 2026 →
  2. 02 Backrooms Your face, cut down the middle and joined to a shifted copy of itself. Whatever is in there is wearing your face. In your browserNeeds a webcam 2026 →
  3. 03 Semantic Stream An AI watches your room through the camera and describes it back to you, a sentence at a time. In your browserWebcam and an AI 2026 →
  4. 04 Lores Film looks and proper pixel art for your photos and videos, including Fujifilm's film simulations. Nothing leaves your browser. There is a Mac app too, with AI photo edits that run on your own machine. Toolpixel.amoghbajpai.com 2026 ↗
  5. 05 Talking A live mirror. The room, your clothes and your hands stay yours, but your face slowly turns into mine. A learning re-build of Aisling Phelan's Surrogate Bodies, made to run live on a MacBook. InstallationRuns on a Mac 2026 ↗
