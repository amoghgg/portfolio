Pixel Manifold

A solo for one face, two hands and 1,298 particles. The audience is the performer: your face and hands are mapped, point by point, into a body of light.

  1. Take the stage. Face and hands enter the field.
  2. Open your hands and the manifold blooms into colour.
  3. Close them and it returns to white.
  4. The meter reads how far you reach.

Runs entirely in your browser. Camera frames never leave your device.
