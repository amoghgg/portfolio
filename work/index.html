Now: rebuilding RRB Apply

Nov 2026 – now · Go, Postgres, AWS
  • The application portal for Indian Railways jobs. Rebuilding rrbapply.gov.in, where candidates register, apply to a recruitment notice, upload documents and pay, as a Go and Postgres system covering 9 recruitment notices. 79 commits so far, in a two-person team. For scale: a single notice, NTPC 2025, drew about 1.22 crore applications for 11,558 posts, roughly a thousand people for every seat, and Group D 2025 drew another 1.08 crore.
  • Identity without leaving the page. Aadhaar OTP e-KYC and DigiLocker sign-in (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, HMAC-checked eAadhaar XML), with a live card comparing what you typed against Aadhaar. Driving-licence numbers are validated down to state, RTO, year and serial.
  • Real OTPs, safely. An SMS and email delivery service with per-recipient rate limits, hCaptcha checked on the server, and an OTP check that verifies as you type without spending the code.
  • Rules ported, then proven. Ported the Go eligibility and fee rules (CEL) to JavaScript for a form simulator of the whole candidate journey. Zero differences on 2,250 random candidates; Playwright runs every notice through to payment and opens all 469 rule dialogs.
  • 33 bugs closed in one day, one commit each, including a rule that never offers SC candidates a zone with no SC seats. Deployed to production with backups, migrations, a written rollback and a separate staging site.

Software Engineer · seQRview

Jan – Nov 2026 · intern May–Aug 2025, converted
  • The identity check every exam operator clears. Aadhaar and driving-licence checks, face liveness, 1:1 selfie-to-ID matching, and hashed-ID deduplication that blocks repeat sign-ups. Roughly 300,000 operators, every check audit-logged.
  • One backend for three apps. Rebuilt seQRview as a single Django and Postgres backend (about 40 models): a dashboard that sets up exams, shifts and centres; an operator app for self-onboarding with Aadhaar KYC; and a field app that confirms the right person reached the right centre, by geofence and face match.
  • Off MongoDB with zero downtime, through a resumable change-stream bridge into Postgres.
  • 4–8 seconds to under 300 ms. Fixed the query patterns behind the main dashboard; CPU fell from about 96% to under 1%, and the smaller server saves about ₹70,000 a year.
  • A shuffle nobody can game. Operators are moved between centres before exam day so nobody knows their posting in advance: min-cost max-flow that maximises dispersion while every centre keeps its exact headcount per role and a single vendor. Ships with a seven-check verifier and a dry-run preview.
  • Run without SSH. Docker with autoscaling and health checks, plus a Telegram bot the team uses to deploy and monitor. No unplanned downtime since launch.

Research

Where machine perception breaks
  1. 01 Morphs Detecting passport photos blended from two faces, from the photo alone. Result: on 10,042 images, a neural net ties a 1990s texture method on clean photos (4.8% vs 5.5% EER) and breaks first under JPEG. Caught three silent bugs by distrusting good results, including 3,532 images leaking into training. 97 tests. Face biometricsFirst pass 2026 →
  2. 02 Lights Five bare-hand gestures fire five DMX lighting cues. Measured by false cues per hour, not clip accuracy, because one stray blackout ruins a show. The live prototype logs per-stage latency and every false trigger. HCI · paperWritten 2026 →
  3. 03 Proof of Place Checking a geotagged photo's place and date against what Sentinel-1 and -2 saw of that field that week, with an honest "can't tell". Preprint and demo planned for March 2027. Satellite imageryIn progress 2026 →
  4. 04 SignFlip Whether AI video codecs that redraw your face change the grammar of sign language. Computer visionOn hold 2026 →

Things I built

Engineering, then the live pieces
  1. 01 Proxy Fraud Detector Flags exam verifications done away from the official desk by looking at the room, not the person: people masked out with DeepLabV3, the background embedded with DINOv2, outliers found per centre. Runs on CPU behind a ranked human-review queue, across about 2M images. Applied MLAt work 2026
  2. 02 Tejas A help bot for RRB Apply candidates in English, Hindi and Hinglish ("otp nahi aa raha"): 77 answers mapped from 50+ real pain points, each citing its source, with live IST deadlines and an age checker. No AI, no network calls, no dependencies; 39 KB, and it runs under a strict CSP. Conversational UXAt work 2026
  3. 03 Face Biometrics API What the commercial face SDKs we buy at work do inside, rebuilt: active liveness (head turns, smile), 1:1 and 1:N matching, behind a containerised FastAPI service. FastAPI · InsightFaceOpen source 2026 ↗
  4. 04 Dispatch Comms Bulk SMS driven by Excel uploads: diffs each upload, queues only the changed rows and sends idempotently, so nobody is texted twice. Replies come back by SMS or a proof-of-delivery photo. FastAPI · CeleryPrivate 2026
  5. 05 Talking A live mirror that repaints your face as mine: TouchDesigner, Stable Diffusion img2img, a fast LoRA and a face LoRA, running live on a MacBook. A credited re-build of Aisling Phelan's Surrogate Bodies. InstallationRuns on a Mac 2026 →
  6. 06 Lores Film looks and real pixel art (Game Boy, PICO-8 and C64 palettes, Floyd–Steinberg or Bayer dithering) for photos and video, computed entirely in the browser with WebGPU. Plus a Mac app with on-device AI edits. ToolLive 2026 ↗
  7. 07 Pixel Manifold Face and hand tracking turned into 1,298 points of light, in the browser. Camera frames never leave the device. In your browserNeeds a webcam 2026 →
