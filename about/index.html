Act three · About · Noida, India

I came to engineering through theatre.

The short version

Before I wrote any serious code, I spent years in theatre. A rehearsal room teaches you things no classroom does: how people move, how timing carries feeling, how one small gesture can change what a whole scene means. I never stopped watching people that way. I just started building with it.

My day job is Assistant Product Manager at Innovatiview. I design and ship the systems that check who someone is: face matching, liveness, signing people in on site, used for staff at national exams across India. So I have watched this technology work, and fail, on cheap phones in bad light, with thousands of real people standing in front of it. I have also built the application portal for a national railway recruitment drive and the tools that run exam days, from question-paper draws to on-site check-in.
